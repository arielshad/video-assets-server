## Space, mostly from NASA

NASA is by far the largest free source of space footage: mission video, astronauts at work, the Sun in extreme ultraviolet, lunar flyovers, galaxies and nebulae, and animations of spacecraft that no camera could film. Wikimedia Commons adds footage from other agencies and observatories under free licences.

## Using it

Space footage carries real rules: credit NASA, keep the NASA logo out of your branding, don't suggest NASA endorses you, and ask before identifiable astronauts appear in an ad. That is why NASA clips carry an editorial flag here. Visualisations of data (the Sun, the Moon, star fields) contain no people or insignia and are the easiest to use as backgrounds.
