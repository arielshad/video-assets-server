## Made for phones

Vertical footage fills a phone screen without cropping, which is exactly what short-form platforms want. Free vertical stock is rarer than landscape, so this collection keeps only clips whose frame is taller than it is wide: NASA's vertical versions of its visualisations, phone-shot clips on Wikimedia Commons and other portrait footage from the sources searched live.

## Using vertical clips

Keep the subject and any text in the middle of the frame: platforms put the caption, account name and buttons along the bottom and right. Check the resolution badge, since many phone uploads are smaller than 1080×1920. Pexels has a large vertical library too, linked from every search.
