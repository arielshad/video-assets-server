## Time, compressed

Timelapses turn slow change into motion: traffic becomes streams of light, a day passes over a skyline, stars wheel overhead, a flower opens. They are the classic opener and transition, and a good background under titles.

## Sources and quality

Public archives hold timelapses from photographers and scientists alike. Wikimedia Commons has many city and sky timelapses under free licences; NASA has Earth seen from the International Space Station as timelapse sequences, credited to NASA. Check for flicker and a steady frame, and the frame rate if you mix them with live footage.
