## The planet as b-roll

Few shots establish "global" faster than Earth seen from orbit: a slow pass over clouds and coastlines, city lights at night, an aurora shimmering over the pole, a rotating globe behind a title. These clips come mostly from NASA, from cameras on the International Space Station and from visualisations built on satellite data.

## Real footage and visualisations

Footage filmed from the space station shows the real view, often as timelapse sequences. Rotating globes, weather and climate animations are visualisations: accurate renderings of data rather than single camera shots. Both are free to use with credit to NASA. Visualisations from the Scientific Visualization Studio come in 1080p and 4K, some at 60 fps.
