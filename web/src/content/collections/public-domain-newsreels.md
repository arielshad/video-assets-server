## News as it was filmed

Newsreels were the cinema's news bulletins from the 1910s to the 1960s: wars and elections, inventions and disasters, sport and fashion. Alongside them, government public-information and documentary films recorded factories, farms, cities and wartime life. Much of this footage is now in the public domain, and the Internet Archive and Wikimedia Commons make it searchable.

## Before you use it

Look at the year, the producer and who stated the licence: footage made by the US government is public domain, while an uploader's public-domain mark on a commercial newsreel needs checking. Expect standard-definition, 4:3, black-and-white transfers with title cards; pillarbox them in a 16:9 edit. Every clip here carries the editorial flag, because real people appear without releases.
