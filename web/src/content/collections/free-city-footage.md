## Cities in an edit

A skyline says where a story happens; a busy street says "now"; light trails at night say "modern". City b-roll opens explainers, business videos and travel pieces, and wide shots cut under almost any voice-over.

## People and signs

Street footage is where licences meet the real world: passers-by, shop signs, branded cars and billboards. That is fine for news, documentary and education. For advertising, pick shots where nobody is recognisable and no brand is prominent, or check each clip's editorial flag. Timelapses and night skylines are usually the safest choices.
